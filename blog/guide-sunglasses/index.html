How to choose sunglasses for the Red Sea sun

Bright sun, sea glare and wind make a difference to what you need from sunglasses in Hurghada. This guide covers the basics so you can choose with confidence.

1. Check UV protection

Look for lenses labelled UV400 (or 100% UV protection). This blocks both UVA and UVB rays. Price does not guarantee this, so ask for the label on the lens or the product sheet.

2. Polarised lenses for sea glare

Polarised lenses reduce the reflected glare from water and sand. They help most when you are on the beach, on a boat or driving along the coast. They can make some phone and car screens hard to read, so check this if it matters to you.

3. Pick a lens colour for your activity

  • Grey or dark brown: good for bright, general use and keeps colours natural.
  • Green: reduces glare and suits sunny, mixed light.
  • Amber or brown: improves contrast on sand and water.

4. Fit matters

Sunglasses that fit well block more light from the sides. Check the bridge, the temple length and the nose pads. If you wear prescription glasses, ask about prescription sunglasses or clip-ons.

5. Frame material

Acetate is light and comes in many colours. Metal frames are thin and light, but check the hinges. Frames that stay in the sun on the dashboard or sand can warp over time, so keep them in a case.
