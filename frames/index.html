Understanding frame sizes

Every frame has three numbers printed inside the temple, for example 52-18-145:

  • 52 · Lens width: the width of one lens in millimetres. Usual range: 50 to 56 mm.
  • 18 · Bridge: the gap between the lenses, which rests on your nose. Usual range: 16 to 22 mm.
  • 145 · Temple length: the length of the arm from the hinge to the tip. Usual range: 135 to 150 mm.

The total width of the frame is roughly the two lenses plus the bridge. A frame that is much wider than your face will sit loosely and stick out at the sides. A frame that is too narrow will press on your temples.

Choosing by face shape

  • Oval: most frame shapes suit you.
  • Round: angular frames (square or rectangular) add definition.
  • Square: round or oval frames soften a strong jaw.
  • Heart: frames that are wider at the bottom, or light rimless styles, balance a wider forehead.
  • Oblong: frames that are tall, or have a decorative top, shorten a long face.
  • Diamond: oval or cat-eye frames that are wider at the eye line suit high cheekbones.
  • Triangle: frames with a stronger top line balance a narrower forehead and wider jaw.

Sizes are a guide. Ask the team at the branch to check the fit before you buy, especially for prescription lenses.
