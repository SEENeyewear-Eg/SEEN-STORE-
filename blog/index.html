    Choosing sunglasses for the Red Sea sun

    Hurghada gets strong sun almost every day of the year, and the glare off the water is strong too. Here is what to check before you buy.

    1. Check the UV protection

    Look for a label that says UV400 or 100% UV protection. This means the lenses block UVA and UVB rays. The colour of the lens does not decide this. A dark lens with no UV protection can make your eyes work harder, because the pupil opens wider.

    2. Polarised does not mean UV protected

    Polarised lenses reduce glare from flat surfaces such as the sea, the road and the car bonnet. They are very useful on the water. But a polarised lens still needs its own UV protection, so check both.

    3. Choose the lens colour for how you use them

    • Grey: keeps colours natural. A good all-day choice.
    • Brown or amber: adds contrast, useful when you drive or spend time outdoors on bright days.
    • Green: a balanced colour, comfortable in mixed light.
    • Mirror coatings: reflect more light, good for very strong sun, but they change how others see your eyes.

    4. Check the fit

    Every frame has three numbers inside the temple, for example 52-18-145: lens width, bridge and temple length, all in millimetres. A frame that is much wider than your face will slide and stick out at the sides. Use the face shape guide to see which frames suit you.

    5. Think about the sea

    Salt, sand and sweat wear down cheap frames quickly. Choose a frame with strong hinges and a comfortable nose pad, and keep the case with you.
